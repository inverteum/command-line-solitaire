Computer players

      
Win rates are completed wins on this 1-card-draw Solitaire ruleset, not generic Klondike. The test used 1,000 random deals, seeds 1–1000, shuffled with Java’s Random.

      
Player 56.8% — 568 / 1,000 games. Player cannot see cards that are not flipped over. It tries several possible layouts for the hidden cards and picks the move that wins most often.

      
Warp 80.8% — 808 / 1,000 games. Warp can see the face-down cards and hidden stock, and it uses that knowledge to pick a move. While Warp is playing, face-down tableau cards are shown.

      
The original hint AI, a baseline that is not one of these two modes, won 386 / 1,000 games (38.6%). Eight of those deals crashed and were counted as losses.

      
In the terminal, type player or warp. AI is a fast Player, and START is a slow Player. Hint stays with the Player.
